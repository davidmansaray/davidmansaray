# David Mansaray

Hi! 👋 I'm a product engineer. I like understanding what's getting in the way and working out what would help.

Often that means building something small enough to learn from, then following the questions it raises.

What will have the most impact on the business? How should it feel to use? What happens behind the scenes when something fails, or the team needs to change it? Bringing those pieces together through design and engineering is what I really enjoy.

I’m curious about what happens after launch, too: how people find a product, start using it and decide to come back.

## Selected work

### Checkout Watch

Checkout monitoring that helps WooCommerce store owners catch problems before customers report them or leave without buying. It checks the journey from adding a product to the basket through to payment selection, recording screenshots and video so the owner can identify the problem and act quickly.

**Technologies:** Laravel · Node.js · Playwright · PostgreSQL.

[Visit the product site](https://checkoutwatch.co/)

### Switchboard

A control room for people and agents managing hire bookings. A booking can change while an agent is working on it. Switchboard keeps drafts, evidence and approvals tied to the current booking, and makes recovery something you can follow.

**Technologies:** Next.js · TypeScript · Mastra · PostgreSQL.

[Try the demo](https://switchboard.davidmansaray.com) · [View source code](https://github.com/davidmansaray/switchboard)

### Perquio

An ambassador platform that helps brands run programmes without juggling spreadsheets, DMs, manual reward tracking and disconnected ecommerce tools. I built it around the full workflow: recruiting ambassadors, reviewing applications, publishing briefs, approving creator submissions, awarding points and fulfilling rewards through WooCommerce.

**Technologies:** Laravel · Vue · Inertia · WooCommerce API.

### Fellwise

An outdoor-experiences marketplace demo. An editor changes a listing and presses Save. When does the booking system see it? Fellwise makes delivery, failures and recovery visible, with the controls to investigate and put things right.

**Technologies:** Laravel · Filament · RabbitMQ · MySQL.

[Try the demo](https://fellwise.davidmansaray.com) · [View source code](https://github.com/davidmansaray/fellwise)

### Tessel

An interactive furniture configurator. It helps shoppers understand how a product could fit their space and preferences before buying, with choices they can see as they make them.

**Technologies:** JavaScript · Three.js · Vite · Blender.

[Try the configurator](https://tessel.davidmansaray.com/)

### EnjoyCrochet

An interactive crochet storefront where shopping and making are part of the same experience. I brought the playful brand to life through a project challenge with progress milestones, a daily wheel for discovering patterns and Loopy, an animated mascot that reacts to what you do. Makers can find their next project, celebrate what they’ve finished and build a personal library, while bundles account for patterns they already own.

**Technologies:** HTML · CSS · JavaScript.

[Try the demo](https://enjoy-crochet.davidmansaray.com) · Proposal prototype with simulated purchases and data.
